Learning something new can feel messy from the inside. You try, you break things, you read the same error message three times, and then one small piece finally makes sense.

I'm trying to remember that the messy middle is part of the work — not proof that I'm failing.

## A note to future me

- Keep showing up, even for 20 minutes.
- Write down what you figured out.
- Ask better questions instead of pretending to know everything.
- Celebrate the fix, not just the finished project.

Progress is allowed to be quiet.
